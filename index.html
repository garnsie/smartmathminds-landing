Over 500 practice questions covering every topic in the Alberta Grade 6 math curriculum. Short answer, multiple choice, and select-all formats — the same formats students encounter on the real PAT. Practice by topic, take full simulations, and track exactly where improvement is needed.

Arcade Game · Multiples & Number Sense

Number Mill

The factory never stops.
Can you keep up?

A classic arcade-style game of sorting multiples in a factory-themed world. Numbers roll down the conveyor belt and you must sort them fast — keep the multiples, discard the rest. Foreman Reed won’t accept any slacking as the difficulty ramps up each round. It’s the kind of practice that doesn’t feel like practice.

  • Sort multiples on a fast-moving conveyor belt
  • Difficulty ramps up each round — keeps students on their toes
  • Daily challenge mode — same board for everyone, worldwide
  • Builds fluency with factors, multiples, and number sense
  • No login, no download — play anywhere, any device

The teacher behind the tools

Hi, I’m Eric.

EG
Eric Garner
Grade 6 Math Teacher · Alberta, Canada

I built Smart Math Minds because the tools I wanted for my own students simply didn’t exist. Free, focused, curriculum-aligned practice for Alberta kids — no ads, no logins, no paywalls. Just good resources that work.

Every tool here started in my own classroom. If it helps my students, I share it. That’s the whole idea.

Smart Math Minds is my corner of the internet for that — a growing collection of games and practice tools built by a teacher, for students. The belief behind all of it is simple: when educators share what works, kids win.

Your support keeps the lights on. Hosting, development, and the time it takes to build things properly aren’t free — but the tools always will be. A coffee or two goes a long way toward making sure that stays true.

Thank you for being here. Whether you donate or just use the tools, you’re part of something that matters to real kids in real classrooms.

— Eric
